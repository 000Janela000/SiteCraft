# SiteCraft — Landing Page Brief

## Purpose
Personal web development service page for Saba Janelidze. Georgian business owners see this after receiving a Facebook message offering web development services. The page must convert visitors into clients.

## Audience
Small-to-medium Georgian business owners who don't have a website. Restaurants, salons, clinics, auto repair shops, etc. Most are not tech-savvy. They need to quickly understand: what you do, why they need it, how much it costs, and how to contact you.

## Language
Georgian (primary). The entire page should be in Georgian. English is NOT needed — the audience is local Georgian businesses.

## Brand
- Name: SiteCraft
- Tone: Professional but approachable. Not corporate. Not overly casual. Think "reliable local expert."
- Colors: Suggest modern, clean palette. Blue/dark tones for trust. Avoid flashy.

## Sections (in order)

### 1. Hero
- Headline: Something like "Your business deserves a website" (in Georgian)
- Subheadline: Brief value prop — "Modern, fast, affordable websites for Georgian businesses"
- CTA button: "See Pricing" or "Contact Me"
- Clean, minimal design. No stock photos of people.

### 2. Why You Need a Website
- 3-4 short cards/blocks explaining benefits:
  - "Customers search for you on Google — if you're not there, they find your competitor"
  - "A website works 24/7, even when you're closed"
  - "Professional image — builds trust with new customers"
  - "Your own space online — not dependent on Facebook's rules"
- Keep it simple. These business owners need to be convinced they need a website at all.

### 3. Pricing
- 4 clear packages in a pricing table/cards:

| Package | Price | What's Included |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Landing Page | from 250 GEL | Single page website, mobile-friendly, modern design |
| Multi-page | from 350 GEL | Multiple pages (Home, About, Services, Contact), mobile-friendly |
| With Admin Panel | from 500 GEL | Everything above + you can edit content yourself without a developer |
| With Payments | from 800 GEL | Everything above + online payment integration for your customers |

- Prices are "starting from" — not fixed. Each project is scoped individually.
- Emphasize: "Agencies charge 2,000+ GEL for the same work"
- Optional: "Free consultation" badge
- Do NOT include free maintenance/support in packages

### 4. Portfolio
- 3 projects:
  - playtime.ge — "Entertainment platform with admin panel"
  - DevNews (dev-news-blond.vercel.app) — "Tech news platform"
  - UniHub (unihub-edu.vercel.app) — "Education platform"
- Show screenshots or mockups of each
- Brief description of what was built

### 5. How It Works
- Simple 3-step process:
  1. "Contact me" — describe what you need
  2. "I build it" — ready in 1-2 weeks
  3. "You're online" — your website is live
- Keep it dead simple. No jargon.

## Design Notes
- Mobile-first — many Georgian business owners will open this on their phone from a Facebook message
- Fast loading — minimal animations, no heavy images
- Georgian typography — use a font that renders Georgian script beautifully
- Dark or light theme — designer's choice, but must feel professional
- No English anywhere on the page — fully Georgian

## Tech Stack
- Static site (no backend needed)
- Deployable to Vercel
- React + Vite or Next.js — match user's existing stack experience
